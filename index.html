Oxblood Winter — product concept

KindHub

Where those who want to help and those who need help gather.

One hub for NGOs seeking volunteers, people with a few minutes to spare, families who need practical help, and anyone who needs human connection or expert guidance — with trust, gamification, and dignity at the center.

How it works What’s inside Try the demo

Who KindHub is for

Three front doors into the same trusted ecosystem.

Foundations & NGOs

Apply for a pool of verified volunteers matched to your current needs — from events to recurring programs.

Volunteers

Browse verified organizations and offers, or pick micro-tasks that fit your schedule — from five minutes to a full shift.

People who need help

Fast “here and now” support, practical errands, or specialized tabs for blind users, elders, large families, and neighbors in crisis.

Help modes

Same app, different urgency and depth — always with safety in mind.

Here & now

Fast human help

For moments that cannot wait: read an expiry date, confirm a label, orient someone in a space. Volunteers nearby or experienced registered helpers can respond first.

Practical care

Groceries, medicine, delivery

For elders, disabled people, parents with many children, or anyone overwhelmed: coordinated shopping, prescriptions, and drop-offs — open to passers-by with 5–15 minutes and to registered volunteers.

For whom · What’s inside

Named spaces so people know where they belong on day one.

“I’m nearby”

For passers-by

Tasks for 5–15 minutes: carry a bag, point the way, quick presence.

“I see for you”

For blind & low-vision users

Video connection (Be My Eyes–style) plus AI assist for text recognition when appropriate.

“Profi”

For specialists

Free consultations: lawyers, psychologists, doctors — verified profiles.

“Funds”

For organizations

Directory of verified NGOs and live needs; volunteer batch requests.

“Market of good”

For everyone

Exchange volunteering credits for partner perks — coffee, shops, services.

Certified helpers (free tier)

Identity-checked access for higher-stakes support.

  • Psychological support — short stabilizing sessions and referrals.
  • Lawyers — benefits, documents, guardianship questions.
  • IT specialists — gadgets, apps, accessibility settings.
  • Tutors — children in large families or residential care.

Gamification & social profile

So volunteers feel seen — not drained.

Digital volunteer passport

Automatic hours and assistance types — exportable for school portfolios and job applications.

Status ladder

From Beginner to Pro, with flavor titles like Navigation Guru for sustained vision-navigation help.

Map of good deeds

Live “lights” for active help requests within about 500 meters — optional, privacy-preserving, and moderated.

Trust & safety

Non-negotiable when elders, children, and medicine are in the story.

Two-way ratings

Volunteers and wards both review after a closed task — fraud-resistant patterns.

SOS

One tap to emergency services or an in-app moderator escalation line.

Verified ID

Government or partner ID checks unlock serious tasks: minors, high-value meds, home visits.

Partners & business

Welcome bonuses that reward verified kindness.

Negotiated perks at cafés and retailers: e.g. five completed verified helps unlock a coffee — reinforcing habit without turning care into a transaction.

Why KindHub

Large portals feel like bureaucracy; scattered social groups feel like chaos. KindHub is a calm, high-trust bridge — micro-kindness and professional depth in one cold-night palette you can actually read at 2 a.m. when someone you love needs help.
