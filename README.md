# KindHub

> 🏆 **Awarded "Most Socially Useful Project"** at the COMTECHNO Hackathon.  
> Official results and team listings: [Announcement in the College Telegram Channel](https://t.me/comtehnokg/226) (look for the *Open Hands* team)

**KindHub** is a conceptual social platform designed to bridge the gap between charitable foundations, volunteers, and individuals in need of urgent or daily assistance.

🔗 [Launch Web Demo (GitHub Pages)](https://nurikor.github.io/KindHub/)

---

## 📌 About the Project (Disclaimer)
This is a hackathon project put together quickly under deadline constraints. It serves as a **demo concept** (MVP) to showcase the interface layout and core logic. The code was written using a pure frontend stack (HTML/CSS/JS) solely for rapid presentation purposes, meaning it is not a finished or production-ready product.

---

## 🔍 The Problem We Solve
* **Foundations** spend a significant amount of time searching for volunteers and verifying individuals who genuinely need aid.
* **Everyday people** want to help out in their spare time but do not know how, or they lack trust in random fundraisers.
* **Those in need** often do not know where to turn and are left to face their struggles alone.

KindHub brings them together into a unified ecosystem of mutual assistance.

---

## 💡 How It Works (Concept)

The platform logic and interface are structured for three core user groups:

### 1. For Foundations
* Finding targeted volunteers and sponsors for specific tasks.
* Initial filtering and verification of incoming aid requests.

### 2. For Everyday Volunteers (Micro-Volunteering)
* An easy way to help out right now if you have an hour of free time (both remotely online or nearby offline).

### 3. For Individuals Seeking Aid
* **Visually Impaired:** A live support feature (inspired by *Be My Eyes*) where volunteers can assist via video or audio calls.
* **Elderly & Large Families:** Everyday assistance requests — such as grocery shopping, running errands, or picking up packages.
* **Fundraising:** To prevent fraud, the platform does not collect money directly. Instead, we verify the person's situation and forward their contact details to trusted partner foundations.
